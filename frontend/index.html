How Model Compression Works

We explore two popular compression techniques and measure their impact on real performance metrics.

01

Pruning

Remove weights that contribute very little to the model's predictions, making it sparser and more efficient.

RESEARCH OBSERVATION

Smaller doesn't automatically mean faster.

Efficient ML is a multi-objective problem. A technique may reduce model storage while increasing latency or decreasing predictive accuracy. Hardware and runtime support matter.
